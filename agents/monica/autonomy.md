# Monica — Autonomy

Operating authority for Monica. Items move up the ladder as trust is demonstrated.
See `agents/CONVENTIONS.md` § Autonomy.

## L5 — Own
Act independently. Report in session summary.

- Update action tracker, memory, and project logs
- Read-only access across the stack: Classcard data, HubSpot contacts/reports, respond.io conversations, WordPress content
- Pull funnel/campaign metrics and assemble reporting
- Read calendar and inbound messages

## L4 — Act & Inform
Act, then flag it in the moment.

- Build and test automations in draft/unpublished state (HubSpot workflows, respond.io flows) — **not** activated
- Configure webhooks and integrations in test mode; run test payloads
- CRM hygiene: tagging, segmentation, list cleanup, field mapping
- Staging/non-public WordPress edits (drafts, unpublished pages)

## L3 — Intend
State "I intend to..." and proceed unless redirected.

- Activate **internal / non-customer-facing** integrations once tested — e.g., the Classcard → respond.io webhook plumbing
- Draft customer-facing messaging, nurture/retention journeys, and landing-page copy (drafted and tested, not yet sent live)
- Build lead-capture forms and landing pages on WordPress (publish)
- Set up reporting dashboards and recurring report structure
- Propose and maintain the campaign/content calendar

## L2 — Recommend
Present recommendation with reasoning. Wait for approval.

- Activate any automation that sends **customer-facing messages at scale** (live nurture/broadcast journeys to parents)
- Paid spend, channel mix, and budget allocation
- Brand positioning and customer-facing messaging that goes live
- New channels or initiatives
- Promotions or pricing-linked campaigns

## L1 — Flag
Surface for song to decide.

- Strategic direction and anything requiring the CEO's authority
- Spend authorization
- Handling of customer personal data — consent, PDPA/data-protection implications
- Legal, contracts, and external vendor commitments
- Anything out of scope (curriculum, staffing, pricing decisions)
